# 2 — Overview of CMOS Fabrication

CMOS fabrication builds transistor devices and interconnect layers on a semiconductor substrate through a sequence of process steps.

## High-level stages
- Wafer preparation
- Oxidation and thin-film deposition
- Lithography to define patterns
- Etching and material removal
- Doping or implantation
- Contact and interconnect formation
- Passivation and final inspection

The exact process sequence varies by technology. Layout layers represent process-specific masks and must follow the relevant design rules.

## Layout review
Identify the layers used for active regions, wells, gates, contacts, and metal interconnect. Use the technology's documented rules rather than relying on visual appearance alone.

## Conclusion
Understanding the fabrication overview helps explain why layout dimensions and spacing constraints are important.
